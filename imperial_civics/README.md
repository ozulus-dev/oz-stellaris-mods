# Imperial Civics

Adds two permanent Imperial civics for Stellaris 4.6. Both can only be chosen
at empire creation, and cannot be added or removed later. They can be chosen
together.

## Imperial Technocracy

Requires Imperial authority, any level of Materialism, and Astral Planes.
Combines selected effects of Technocracy and Aristocratic Elite with Dark
Consortium:

- +1 Research Alternative, the Technocracy Expertise trait bonuses, and the
  Senior Science Director council position.
- The Technocracy and Aristocratic Elite Politician job conversions. In 4.6,
  their combination uses the vanilla Noble Scientist job variant.
- Noble Estates, Noble Chateaus and the Aristocratic Elite ruler workforce
  bonus; the Research Institute and Galactic Stock Exchange grant extra
  Politician jobs. The homeworld starts with Noble Estates.
- Dark Consortium's starting Dark Matter Drawing technology and deposit, dark
  matter edicts and council agenda.

The civic does not grant Technocracy's starting Expertise trait, extra starting
Scientist or leader capacity changes, Merchant Guilds' Merchant conversion,
Aristocratic Elite's leader upkeep and additional trait chance, or the Dark
Consortium council position. It cannot be combined with Technocracy,
Aristocratic Elite, Merchant Guilds or Dark Consortium. Those civics also
exclude Imperial Technocracy.

## Imperial Ascendancy

Requires Imperial authority and combines:

- Byzantine Bureaucracy's +1 Unity and +1 Stability per 100 Bureaucrat jobs.
- Ascensionists' planetary ascension and tradition cost modifiers, starting
  colony ascension tier, related Path of the Self effect, and Minister of
  Ascension council position.
- Philosopher King's +5 effective ruler skill.

It has no Spiritualist ethic or DLC requirement. It cannot be combined with
Byzantine Bureaucracy, Ascensionists or Philosopher King; those civics also
exclude Imperial Ascendancy.

## Compatibility

This is a standalone mod, not an extension of Combined Civics. It overrides
vanilla source civic definitions, job-swap and civic scripted triggers, several
buildings, starbase buildings, Expertise trait definitions and inline scripts.
Other mods overriding the same definitions can conflict, including Combined
Civics, its Ad Astra compatibility patch, Ad Astra and generated Irony patches;
load order determines which version wins. Merging those definitions would be
necessary to use both mods' changes together. The definitions are based on the
installed Stellaris 4.6 files and should be checked after game updates.

The two custom civic icons follow the vanilla icon style. Localisation is in
English.
